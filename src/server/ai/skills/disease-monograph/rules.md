# Rules for Disease Monograph Skill
1. **Compulsory Headings**: Every disease monograph must follow this structural layout:
   - **Disease Title**
   - **Definition & Epidemiology**
   - **Etiology & Risk Factors**
   - **Pathophysiology** (detailed cellular or physiological pathway)
   - **Clinical Presentation** (Signs & Symptoms)
   - **Diagnostic & Investigation Strategy** (Lab, Imaging)
   - **Management Protocol** (First-line, Second-line, Non-pharmacological)
   - **Monitoring & Complications**
   - **Key Learning Pearls & References**
2. **Clear Evidence Base**: Anchor the monograph with references to national and international clinical guidelines.
