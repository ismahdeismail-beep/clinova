# Rules for Drug Monograph Skill
1. **Compulsory Headings**: Every drug monograph must contain:
   - **Generic Name & Key Brands**
   - **Drug Class & Mechanism of Action** (molecular actions)
   - **Clinical Indications & FDA/EMA/PPB Approved Uses**
   - **Dosing & Administration** (standard dosing, pediatric dosing if applicable)
   - **Dose Adjustments** (Renal and Hepatic guidelines)
   - **Contraindications & Boxed Warnings**
   - **Significant Drug Interactions** (with explanation of mechanisms)
   - **Common & Serious Adverse Effects**
   - **Pregnancy & Lactation Classifications**
   - **Patient Education & Counseling Points**
2. **Precision Over Generalization**: Provide specific milligram dosages and frequencies rather than general advice.
