1. Mr. Barasa presents to you with symptoms of cough, wheezing, dyspnea and chest tightness for one month. He reports that the symptoms occur daily and interfere with his ability to go to work or even carry out normal routine activities at home. He is a road works contractor and is currently involved in the construction of a new highway in his county. He reports night time symptoms twice every week and has to wake up to use his inhaler. Besides the inhaler which he was told by is doctor to use whenever he experiences the symptoms of wheezing and dyspnea, he also reports using salbutamol tablets and prednisolone tablets. He however reports that he does not use them consistently because of the tremors and palpitations that he experiences. He has come to you because last night, the inhaler was not able to relieve the symptoms and he had a troublesome night.
What condition is Mr. Barasa suffering from? (2 Marks)
