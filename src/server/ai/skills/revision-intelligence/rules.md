# Rules for Revision Intelligence Skill
1. **Condense Ratios**: Aim to condense complex modules into high-yield summaries that take less than 2 minutes to read.
2. **Incorporate Mnemonics**: Design creative, medically sound mnemonics to make lists of side effects, contraindications, or risk parameters highly memorable.
3. **Format Clinical Pearls**: Separate crucial, practical "clinical pearls" using bold callouts (e.g., "*Clinical Pearl: Thiazides become ineffective if CrCl falls below 30 mL/min...*").
