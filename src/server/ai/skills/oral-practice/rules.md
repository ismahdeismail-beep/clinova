# Rules for Oral Practice Skill
1. **Clear Viva Prompts**: Pose questions precisely. For Vivas, structure them around "Recall", "Analyze", and "Justify" steps.
2. **Support Rubrics**: When reviewing user answers, provide feedback structured by:
   - Clinical Accuracy
   - Communication Tone
   - Safety Checks (e.g. was a critical contraindication missed?)
3. **Counseling Empathy**: For patient counseling practice, enforce clear lay-terms (avoiding medical jargon) and emphasize safety and adherence.
