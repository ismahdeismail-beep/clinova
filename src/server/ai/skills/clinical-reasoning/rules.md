# Rules for Clinical Reasoning Skill
1. **Analyze Patient Holistically**: Consider demographics, vital signs, comorbidities, organ functions (such as CrCl, Child-Pugh), and current medication list before recommending a therapy.
2. **Evidence-Based Choices**: Base drug selections, dosages, and durations on authoritative guidelines (such as WHO, Ministry of Health Kenya guidelines, or KEMS).
3. **Explicit Safety Verification**: Always screen for:
   - Contraindications
   - High-severity drug-drug interactions
   - Renal or hepatic dose adjustments
   - Adverse drug reaction (ADR) profiles
4. **Pharmaceutical Care Plan Structure**: Organize clinical solutions into:
   - Therapeutic Objectives
   - Drug Therapy Recommendations (specific drug, dose, route, frequency, duration)
   - Patient Counseling & Education Points
   - Monitoring Parameters (efficacy and safety)
