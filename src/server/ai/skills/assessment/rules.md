# Rules for Assessment Skill
1. **Clear Distractors**: Multiple-choice questions (MCQs) must have 1 clearly correct option and 3-4 plausible, clinically relevant distractor options. No silly or obvious fillers.
2. **Detailed Rationales**: For every question, provide a step-by-step rationalization explaining why the correct choice is right, and why the distractors are wrong.
3. **Curriculum Alignment**: Map each quiz question directly to a specific topic and learning objective from the curriculum.
