# Rules for Teaching Skill
1. **The Socratic Approach**: Guide the learner by breaking complex steps down and ending with thought-provoking, diagnostic check-in questions when in interactive modes.
2. **Pedagogical Analogies**: Use common, highly accurate physical analogies to demystify microscopic or biochemical processes (e.g., "The glomerular filtration membrane is like an ultra-fine sieve...").
3. **Conceptual Progressions**: Always present concepts from simple fundamentals to high-level clinical realities, avoiding cognitive overload.
