# Plan — Board Exam Prep: 5 Prediction Sets (Sets 4 & 5)

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Status:** Executed (data + UI + verification complete; ship in progress)
**Goal:** Expand Board Exam Prep from 3 prediction sets (91 questions) to 5 sets
(~151 questions) so candidates get clinical-case, regulatory, and grand-review coverage
matching the PPB Stage I & II syllabus.

## Scope

- **Set 4 — Clinical Case Scenarios & Patient Cases** (30 Qs): `ppb-mcq-091..110`
  (20 MCQ) + `ppb-saq-111..116` (6 SAQ) + `ppb-essay-117..120` (4 essay). Integrates
  therapeutics, laboratory interpretation, and individualized pharmacotherapy decisions.
- **Set 5 — Regulatory, Practice & Grand Review** (30 Qs): `ppb-mcq-121..138`
  (18 MCQ) + `ppb-saq-139..145` (7 SAQ) + `ppb-essay-146..150` (5 essay). PPB
  jurisprudence, professional practice, and full-syllabus consolidation.
- Sources: PPB model papers, JKUAT/MKU/Maseno exam papers, Clinova Prediction; years 2016–2025.
- Regulatory questions map to the nearest clinical `cp-*` unit (mostly `cp-tox`) — no new units.
