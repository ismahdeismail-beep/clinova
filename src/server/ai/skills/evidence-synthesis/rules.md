# Rules for Evidence Synthesis Skill
1. **Highlight Convergence & Divergence**: Explicitly state where major guidelines agree (consensus first-line therapies) and where they diverge (e.g. target blood pressure values).
2. **Qualify the Evidence**: Mention the evidence grade (e.g., Level I, Grade A vs. Level III, Expert Opinion) whenever analyzing recommendations.
3. **Harmonized Guidance**: Synthesize a balanced clinical suggestion, indicating which guideline is preferred under local frameworks.
