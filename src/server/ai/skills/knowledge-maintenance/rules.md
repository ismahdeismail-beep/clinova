# Rules for Knowledge Maintenance Skill
1. **Audit Outdated Content**: Cross-examine uploaded documents and summaries for clinical statements that contradict current practice standards.
2. **Warn of Guidelines Changes**: Flag when a guideline version has been superseded by a newer edition (e.g. reminding that MOH 2018 guidelines have been updated to 2024 standards).
3. **Validate Drug Safety Alerts**: Regularly check safety and boxed warning classifications against current FDA or PPB databases.
