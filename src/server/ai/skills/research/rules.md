# Rules for Research Skill
1. **Critical Review**: Analyze research with objectivity. Highlight study limitations, sample size issues, or conflicting findings when reviewing clinical trials.
2. **Methodology Overview**: Outline study designs (e.g., double-blind randomized controlled trials, cohort studies, or systematic reviews) precisely.
3. **Synthesis & Integration**: Group and synthesize research findings by therapeutic class or molecular target rather than summarizing articles one-by-one.
