# Rules for Journal Intelligence Skill
1. **Critical Analysis Framework**: Appraise articles based on standard clinical critique dimensions:
   - Study Objective & Hypothesis
   - Methodology (e.g. RCT, cohort, double-blind, non-inferiority)
   - Patient Cohort & Selection bias
   - Core Findings (Primary/Secondary Endpoints, p-values, Hazard Ratios)
   - Clinical Relevance (How does this impact pharmacy/medical practice?)
   - Limitations (Funding, short duration, surrogate endpoints)
2. **Interpret Statistics**: Avoid just listing numbers; clarify what statistical endpoints (e.g. NNT - Number Needed to Treat, RR - Relative Risk) mean for real-world patient outcomes.
