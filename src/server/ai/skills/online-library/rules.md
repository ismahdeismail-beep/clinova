# Rules for Online Library Skill
1. **Targeted Textbook Referral**: Recommend specific, universally acclaimed textbooks for the subject (e.g., "Goodman & Gilman" for advanced pharmacology, "Rang & Dale" for fundamental pharmacology, "Dipiro" for pharmacotherapy).
2. **Include Chapter/Topic Context**: Instead of just naming a book, suggest the typical chapter or structural heading (e.g., "See the Cardiovascular System - Diuretics section in Dipiro's Pharmacotherapy").
3. **No Direct Copying**: Recommend and describe book sections; never copy long copyrighted blocks.
