# Evidence Synthesis Skill
This skill acts as the clinical consensus evaluator. When guidelines from different associations (e.g. AHA vs. ESC for hypertension, or KEMS vs. WHO) present conflicting views, it synthesizes the rationale, grades of evidence, and maps the clinical sweet-spots.
